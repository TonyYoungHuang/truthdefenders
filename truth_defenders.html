Common Misinformation Tactics

Manipulated Media

Edited images and videos taken out of context or altered to create false narratives.

Repetition & Scale

False claims repeated so often they begin to sound true, amplified across social media platforms.

Fake Experts

Using unqualified individuals presented as experts to legitimize false or misleading claims.
